# Environments & Infrastructure

Artsgoz services run primarily on Google Cloud Platform (GCP). Application code and infrastructure definitions are maintained in the [`artsgoz/artsgoz-monorepo`](https://github.com/artsgoz/artsgoz-monorepo).

Use the pages in this section to understand how the platform is organized:

- [Environments](./environments) describes the development, staging, and production environments and lists their service URLs.
- [Infrastructure Architecture](./infrastructure) describes the hosting platforms, deployment pipeline, and infrastructure management approach.

# Access to GCP

To access gcp projects, you need to contact PM or [@teruyukii](https://github.com/teruyukii) to provision your `@artsgoz.com` account first.
