# Way of Working

### Communication

We use <mark>Artsgoz Tech discord</mark> channel for internal communication.

- **Access**: If you have not yet joined the server or are missing access to specific channels, please reach out to your Project Manager (PM) to receive an invite and the appropriate roles.
- **Guidelines**: Use public channels for project-related discussions to maintain transparency, and keep threads organized for specific technical topics.

### Process/Workflow

We follow an Agile methodology with a 2-week sprint cycle to ensure our development remains iterative, transparent, and adaptable. Our standard sprint routine includes:

> {: .note }
> Maple, please check and update this krub.

- **Sprint Planning**: Held at the start of the sprint week to define our goals, discuss priorities, and assign tasks.
- **Standup Updates**: Conducted **mid-sprint** to share progress, identify any blockers, and ensure the team is aligned.
- **Sprint Review**: Held at the end of the sprint (Friday or Saturday) to review completed work and discuss what we can improve for the next cycle.

### Task Tracking

We use GitHub Projects as our single source of truth for task management, bug tracking, and sprint planning.

- **Project Board**: [Artsgoz GitHub Project](https://github.com/orgs/artsgoz/projects/3)
- **Ticket Lifecycle**: All team members are expected to keep their assigned tickets updated. When picking up a task, assign yourself and move the ticket through the appropriate columns (e.g., To Do → In Progress → In Review → Done).
